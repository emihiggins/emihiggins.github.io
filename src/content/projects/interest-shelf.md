Your interests deserve more than a forgotten bookmarks folder.

Interest Shelf is a place to save worthwhile links — articles, videos, tools,
podcasts, odd corners of the web — shape them into a focused collection, and publish
that shelf on a site you already own. The shelf keeps changing as your interests do,
without another deploy.

## Publish anywhere

Every published collection gets a clean hosted page plus portable formats, so it can
live wherever you already are: a script tag, an iframe, an RSS feed, or a JSON API. No
rebuild required — any platform that accepts custom HTML or an iframe can host a shelf,
and plain HTML and React sites are tested directly.

## Share deliberately

Visibility is per collection, and individual entries can stay hidden regardless:

- **Private** stays owner-only.
- **Unlisted** stays out of profiles and search indexing.
- **Public** appears on your profile and in the open formats.

Analytics count daily views and clicks without tracking people across the web.

## Status

Free private beta — deliberately small, focused on learning from a handful of users
before any pricing decisions. Sign-in is via GitHub.
